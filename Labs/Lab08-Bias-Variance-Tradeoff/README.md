# Lab 08 – Bias-Variance Tradeoff

## Overview
In this lab, I explored the bias-variance tradeoff and how it relates to underfitting, overfitting, and a model's ability to generalize to new data.

## What I Did
I examined how model complexity can affect training and testing performance and worked through examples involving bias and variance.

## What I Learned
I learned that a model can perform poorly if it is too simple or too complex. High bias can lead to underfitting, while high variance can lead to overfitting. The goal is to find a balance that allows the model to learn meaningful patterns while still performing well on unseen data.

## Challenges
The relationship between bias, variance, underfitting, and overfitting was initially challenging to connect. Working through the examples made it easier to understand how model complexity affects performance.
