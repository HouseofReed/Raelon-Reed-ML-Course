# Lab 11 – Hyperparameter Tuning and AutoML

## Overview
In this lab, I explored hyperparameter tuning and automated machine learning (AutoML) as methods for improving and streamlining the model-building process.

## What I Did
I practiced adjusting model settings and examined how different hyperparameter choices can affect model performance. I also explored the role of automation in testing machine learning configurations.

## What I Learned
I learned that hyperparameters are settings chosen before or during model training that can significantly affect performance. Tuning provides a systematic way to search for stronger configurations, while AutoML can automate parts of model selection and optimization.

## Challenges
One challenge was understanding the difference between model parameters learned from data and hyperparameters selected by the developer. The lab helped make that distinction clearer and showed why tuning should be performed systematically.
