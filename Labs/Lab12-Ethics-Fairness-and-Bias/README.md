# Lab 12 – Ethics, Fairness, and Bias

## Overview
In this lab, I examined ethical issues in machine learning, with a focus on fairness and bias in data and model outcomes.

## What I Did
I explored how bias can enter a machine learning system and considered the impact that data, model design, and evaluation decisions can have on different groups.

## What I Learned
I learned that strong model performance does not automatically mean a machine learning system is fair or responsible. Developers also need to consider where the data came from, who may be affected by the model, and whether outcomes are distributed fairly.

## Challenges
One challenge was recognizing that bias can occur at multiple stages of the machine learning process rather than only in the final model. This lab helped me think more critically about the responsibilities involved in developing AI systems.
